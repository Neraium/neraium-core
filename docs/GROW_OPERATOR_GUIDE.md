# GROW OPERATOR GUIDE

At every scope, focus on:

1. Current state
2. Dominant relationship shift
3. Cross-system driver
4. Inefficiency summary
5. Biological risk
6. Estimated time to impact
7. Recommended action

The preferred hero sentence is:

`This entity is drifting relative to its expected structural behavior and its peers, primarily due to X, with likely impact in Y time unless action is taken.`

Grow is advisory only. It does not control equipment or automate actuation.
